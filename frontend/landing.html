Standard Operating Procedure

From detection to resolution

A disciplined, nine-stage operational progression that ensures every road event is captured, analyzed, remediated, and tracked to closure.

STAGE 01 Intake

Detect

Field inspections, operator observations, or citizen reports record defects and road hazards with GPS coordinates and classification.

STAGE 02 Telemetry

Monitor

Corridor telemetry captures vehicle volume, average velocities, slowdown patterns, and traffic flow changes in the affected vicinity.

STAGE 03 Evaluation

Analyze

System correlates the reported defect with traffic density and violation occurrences to understand corridor impact.

STAGE 04 Calculation

Assess Risk

Deterministic weighting algorithm computes a standardized risk index (0–100) based on emergency signals, road damage, and congestion.

STAGE 05 Escalation

Notify

Operational alerts are routed to responsible duty operators, road inspectors, or emergency coordinators based on role privileges.

STAGE 06 Tasking

Dispatch

A formal Work Order is generated with priority level, assigned crew, target location, and strict SLA target.

STAGE 07 Field Work

Execute

Field crews deploy to the scene, conduct repairs (patching, barrier repair, debris clearance), and update operational status to In Progress.

STAGE 08 Completion

Resolve

Work is verified on site by the designated road inspector, completing the work order and clearing associated incident flags.

STAGE 09 Audit

Track

Resolution timestamps, assigned crew performance metrics, SLA compliance rates, and operational records are archived into permanent system audit logs.

Access & Permissions

Clear responsibilities across the operation

Road System Control enforces structured Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) to ensure operational authority matches municipal responsibilities.

ROLE: ADMIN

System Administrator

System administration, user account management, role assignment, configuration parameters, and comprehensive audit trail inspection.

ROLE: TRAFFIC OPERATOR

Traffic Operator

Corridor traffic monitoring, telemetry review, congestion threshold alerts, and traffic infraction recording.

ROLE: EMERGENCY OPERATOR

Emergency Operator

Emergency incident triage, immediate priority broadcast, incident lifecycle management, and emergency team dispatch.

ROLE: ROAD INSPECTOR

Road Inspector

Road issue field inspection, photographic defect logging, work order remediation tracking, and on-site repair verification.
